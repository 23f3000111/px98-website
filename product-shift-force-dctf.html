PX98 SHIFT FORCE DCTF

Engineered to withstand the challenging and extreme demands of modern wet dual clutch transmissions, PX98 SHIFT FORCE DCTF delivers exceptional high-torque performance, instantaneous gear engagement, superior clutch holding capability, and remarkable shear and thermal stability. Its state-of-the-art synthetic formulation ensures maximum power transfer, superior anti-shudder characteristics, outstanding oxidation resistance, and formidable wear protection under continuous high-load and high-temperature operations.
