PX98 ENGINE PERFORMANCE TREATMENT

Formulated with advanced friction modifier technology, PX98 ENGINE PERFORMANCE TREATMENT enhances the performance of engine oil by reducing internal mechanical friction, strengthening lubricating oil film and viscosity integrity, and providing additional anti-wear protection under both daily and demanding driving conditions. Its state-of-the-art formulation promotes smoother engine operation, improved fuel economy, improved high-temperature lubrication stability, and reduced metal-to-metal wear while maintaining compatibility with all kinds of engine oils.
