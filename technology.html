Protecting the Engine and the Exhaust

Modern Euro VI diesels run DPF, DOC, SCR and EGR systems that conventional high-ash chemistry will foul. PX98 heavy duty oils use low and reduced-SAPS additive technology so soot handling, piston cleanliness and extended drain capability arrive without shortening after-treatment life.
