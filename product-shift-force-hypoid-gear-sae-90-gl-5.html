PX98 SHIFT FORCE HYPOID GEAR SAE 90 GL-5

Developed for medium- to heavy-duty hypoid gear units, PX98 SHIFT FORCE HYPOID GEAR SAE 90 GL-5 utilizes premium and carefully selected base stocks and advanced extreme-pressure additive system to deliver optimized level of load-carrying performance, dependable thermal-oxidative stability, robust wear protection, and long-lasting lubrication. It effectively shields hypoid and differential gears operating under low-speed, high torque and severe shock-loading conditions.
