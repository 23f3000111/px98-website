PX98 SHIFT FORCE CVTF

Developed for modern continuously variable transmissions, PX98 SHIFT FORCE CVTF provides optimized friction characteristics, optimum anti-shudder performance, superior oxidation stability, and exceptional wear protection. Its premium synthetic formulation ensures smooth acceleration, maximum transmission efficiency, improved fuel economy performance, and long-lasting reliability for belt and chain-driven CVTs across generation.
