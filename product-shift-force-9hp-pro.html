PX98 SHIFT FORCE ATF 9HP PRO

Dedicated-built blue fluid for the latest generation of 9HP 9-speed automatic transmissions produced by ZF, PX98 SHIFT FORCE 9HP PRO is engineered using cutting-edge fully synthetic base oils with next-generation low-viscosity technology to deliver exceptionally rapid and seamless gear engagement, precise shift control, optimized thermal stability, and maximum wear protection. Its state-of-the-art additive formulation provides excellent shear stability and optimized hydraulic performance while maintaining smooth, responsive shifting and allow the highest transmission efficiency under demanding driving conditions such as high torque loads.
