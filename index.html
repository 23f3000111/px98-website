Four engineering priorities
Film integrity

Advanced Molecular Technology

Leveraging leading-edge molecular technology, every PX98 formulation is engineered to optimise lubricant performance, reinforce oil film integrity, strengthen engine protection and deliver utmost reliability under the demands of modern mobility.

Friction

Superior Friction Management

Precision-engineered friction management additive technologies help reduce mechanical losses, protect critical engine components, and maximise long-term engine efficiency and energy conserving.

Thermal

Enhanced Thermal Performance

Maintains lubricant stability and viscosity integrity under elevated operating temperatures, delivering consistent and robust engine protection when performance matters most.

Cleanliness

Intelligent Contaminant Control

Advanced detergent and dispersant technologies are fortified to effectively manage contaminants, helping maintain lubricant cleanliness throughout challenging operating conditions.

Today's lubrication technology

Precision Starts at the Molecular Level.

Every PX98 premium engine oil begins with carefully selected premium base stocks and highly-advanced additive systems.

The result is quicker lubrication during cold starts, stronger oil film under extreme loads, outstanding shear stability, and formidable oxidation resistance that keeps today's turbocharged engines running at peak efficiency.
