Technical articles

Low speed pre-ignition, explained

In a downsized turbocharged direct-injection engine at low revs and full load, the charge can light before the spark does. The pressure spike that follows is violent enough to break a piston ring land. Oil chemistry is part of the cause and part of the answer, which is why API SP and ILSAC GF-6 both test for it.

Technical articles

GDI & TGDI engines: lubrication challenges

Spraying fuel straight into the cylinder keeps it off the intake valves it used to wash clean, so deposits build where nothing scrubs them. Add a turbocharger and the oil meets higher temperatures, more soot and fuel dilution at once. Low volatility, piston cleanliness and timing chain wear protection are what a modern formulation has to answer with.

Maintenance

Should automatic transmission fluid be changed?

“Filled for life” means the life the manufacturer warrants, not the life of the car. ATF shears, oxidises and loses its friction characteristics with heat, and a shudder or a late shift is usually the fluid rather than the gearbox. Towing, traffic and hot climates all shorten the interval, and matching the OEM specification on the refill matters more than the badge.
