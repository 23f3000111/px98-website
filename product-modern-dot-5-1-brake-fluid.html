PX98 MODERN DOT 5.1 BRAKE FLUID

Engineered for modern high-performance braking systems, PX98 MODERN DOT 5.1 BRAKE FLUID combines premium synthetic borate ester technology with outstanding low-temperature fluidity to deliver exceptionally fast hydraulic response, superior pedal consistency, and outstanding resistance to vapor lock under extreme braking conditions. Its advanced formulation provides exceptional thermal stability, excellent corrosion protection, and reliable braking performance for vehicles equipped with ABS, ESC, ESP, and advanced electronic braking systems.
